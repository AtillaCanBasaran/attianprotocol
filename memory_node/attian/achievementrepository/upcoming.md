How I would recover these memories
Instead of trying to remember your entire childhood chronologically, pick one environment at a time: your first bedroom, the family kitchen, the school playground, the car during a holiday, a grandparent's house, an old gaming setup.

Then try to reconstruct what you did there, who was present, and which small incidents became stories. Ask family members for their versions, too. Mark uncertain dates and details as reconstructed rather than confirmed. Childhood memory has questionable version control.

The biggest design decision: don't make legendary achievements synonymous with socially impressive achievements.

Getting into as good school can be legendary. So can the first time you rode a bicycle alone. So can an evening when the entire family laughed at something nobody remembers anymore.

Attian Protocol should preserve all three, because the archive isn't measuring success by society's standards. It's preserving what made the Subject's life his own.

The trick is that some of these achievements will only become legendary after you remember them.


The Achievement Repository should reward things that actually happened, including things the Subject never intended to achieve. That's what separates it from Mission Log, which tracks unfinished quests and future ambitions.
I'd introduce these achievement classes:
LEGENDARY
Life-changing events and major personal milestones

RARE
Unusual experiences, discoveries, and unlikely victories

COMMON
Small but meaningful achievements of everyday life

SECRET
Accidental, embarrassing, bizarre or retrospectively discovered unlocks

CURSED
Achievements that technically count, despite unfortunate consequences
