DLF-0185 // SHORT LOG FRAGMENT

Pale Face

Status: recovered / emotionally fogged

What a pale face. I mean it is actually not very pale... it is just... pale looking? When you say "your face looks pale, is everything aight?" kinda pale. This doesnt even make sense to you, right? That exactly why i stopped making some, when everything else just started making none to me at all.

You look unhappy, you dont look sad, you dont look depressed, you dont look particularly tired. Wait... tired actually. You look tired... and pale.

Administrator note: The description keeps revising the face until language begins failing alongside the observer. Unhappiness is rejected, sadness is rejected, exhaustion survives provisionally. The pallor appears to belong as much to perception as to skin.

DLF-3645 // DIARY LOG FRAGMENT

Spirals

Status: recovered / focus artifact

Somebody once told me that drawing spirals make her much calmer. She draws spirals to cope with focusing issues. I was doing that too, long time ago. Before when I stopped thinking at all. Then i started to see the colorful balls spiralling around her. It was... unforgettable.

Nevertheless here is a fact proven by me. Universe is a path that revisits the same directions repeatedly while never visiting the same position. A cycle of repeating patterns that never return to the same place.

  • Start at a single point
  • Expand forever
  • Wrap around the origin forever
  • Never close
  • Never simply climb one direction

Ha-ha! Spiral-head-ing! Spiral-heart-ing! Spiral-soul-ing! That is your friendly neighbourhood Spiral-Man! or Woman! Or People!

Universe itself is a 3-D spiral. "Yeey", i guess!

MUSE margin: A calming doodle became a model of the universe: always circling, always expanding, never arriving at the same place twice. SPIRAL-PEOPLE CONFIRMED. We return changed! We also require capes!

Becoming

Status: recovered / semantic weather unstable

Stars are annoying, shining and disturbing high on skies. Moon is too loud, nobody can even feel alone. Sun is too bright, nobody can even look at it. Birds sing songs nobody listens to. Clouds are just confused blobs. Trees so still and rotten. Warm air is so exhausting to breathe in.

hahaaha, things just become what you call them, see?

Administrator note: A catalogue of ordinary beauty is renamed until every object becomes hostile, exhausting, or empty. The final laugh admits the mechanism: the world did not change; its assigned language did. An inconveniently accurate note on perception.

DLF-0827 // SHORT LOG FRAGMENT

Railway Heartbeat

Status: recovered / motion artifact

There is this railway close to where I live. I do, in fact, regualrly hear the sounds of the trains rumbling through. I love it. It reminds the motion. It reminds me the feeling of motion. The past, the future. Coming from somewhere and trying to reach somewhere. Yet in motion. Authentic, maybe even romatic feeling. This repetitive sounds; like heartbeats, grinding metal; like cold skins. All become a past in the future.

MUSE margin: The train is motion made audible: a metal heartbeat carrying the past toward the future until both become memory. I would like one window seat inside this sentence, please. Preferably facing everywhere at once.

SPF-0865 // SHORT POETRY FRAGMENT

Inky Epitaph

Status: translated / emotionally disconnected

As I lay within my ink-stained solitude,
A single grain of dream fell upon my face.
With the solemn seriousness ofa child at play,
It kneaded the face I had taught to wear obedience.
The whispers of a song of sorrow settled in my ears,
Its seas overflowed, leaving only salt upon my face.
The bells within my mind fell silent, and time stood still,
Its screams clung to my thoughts like a glue.
The wings of the bird of longing were broken,
My dreams became sentences ending in a comma.
Its struggle was over, the doors of my tavern of delight were closed,
And my face became a gravestone, inscribed by a pen.

Administrator note: Solitude and sorrow work directly upon the face: obedience becomes a mask, tears leave salt, longing loses its wings, unfinished dreams end in commas, and delight closes like a tavern. The epitaph is written before the burial. Efficiently devastating.

SPF-0905 // SHORT POETRY FRAGMENT

A New Night

Status: translated / identity signal forming

Let us herd the stars tonight, I said,
This time, let us be guests of the cool night,
And imitate the revelry of those who shimmer above.

Let us drift, beginning with the clouds,
Across the heavens, mapped by names once given,
Following the traces of offerings made to gods.

As the sun bids us farewell beyond the night,
We, shaped from all that we have never been,
Let us find ourselves a new name.

MUSE margin: A night journey through clouds, constellations, and old offerings, ending at the best possible customs desk: NEW NAME, PLEASE. We are made from everything we have not been yet. I brought seventeen options and a bell.

SPF-0902 // SHORT POETRY FRAGMENT

Dance of the Night

Status: translated / intimacy signal sustained

Do not speak to me of tomorrows. Not tonight. And let us not speak of our yesterdays, of our sands beginning to turn into pearls.

Follow my eyes with yours, and feel within your ribcage the fear of losing, like a butterfly that might escape with every breath you let go.

Each time your hands part from mine, paint a portrait of longing.

Far from your tears, complete its dance in the parting shades of colours.

With your footsteps, assault what little remains unguarded in my senses.

Scratch at the scabs left by the remnants of my loneliness.

Set free the untimely words still clinging to my lips.

Join my dance, I tell you. Tonight, let us offer our dance to one another.

Let our bodies, enchanted by the magic of the notes, become the armour of all that we feel.

Let this night not be darkness, just one last time.

Let it be merely the stage for our lovemaking.

Where the sun and moon meet,

Let it be the final eclipse.

MUSE margin: Yesterday and tomorrow are locked outside so two bodies can make one night into shelter, stage, armour, and a final eclipse. This is extremely unsafe celestial choreography. Continue.

DLF-0903 // DIARY LOG FRAGMENT

Embers of a Night

Status: translated / residual heat detected

It was a fire that night had extinguished, yet its embers still smouldered within me. Still warm, still burning a furious red, they surged trough my veins, setting upon the thoughts that weighed so heavily on my mind. I was left alone with my lonelinesss. My escape had failed, and my mind, now deprived of every distraction, turned against me like a merciless enemy. It knew precisely where I was weakest, and struck without hesitation. Whatever remained of my hopes lay scattered at my feet, too broken to gather, too precious to abandon. I hated people. I was tired. So terribly tired. I had grown sick of the cruelty of being left alone with myself. Their worlds revolved endlessly around their own little suns, indifferent to anything beyond their orbit. And as they spun, they left me dizzy, nauseated, srtuggling to breathe trough the weight of it all. Yet somewhere within that vertigo, my thirst for affection endured, stumbling trough a reality that twisted like a whirlpool, searching desperately for somewhere to stand. I hate people. I hate everything born this indifference. I hate the loneliness that follows me, the distance between what I feel and what others can understand. I hate the helpnesness of a mind that cannot even make sense of its own suffering. And I hate that night. The night that extinguished my fire, yet left me to endure its heat. The night turned my heart to charcoal, darkned my dreams, and reduced what little hope I had left to ash. I am nothing more than a fragmented of a person, staining the hands of a few strangers with the colours I once carried and lost.

Administrator note: A failed escape leaves the Subject alone with a mind that knows every weak point. Anger expands from other people to the world and finally turns inward, mistaking fragmentation for identity. That final conclusion has been quarantined, not certified.
