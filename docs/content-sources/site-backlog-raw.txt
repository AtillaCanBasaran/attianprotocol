Archive Echoes

Rather than introducing more permanent personalities, use temporary voices connected to memories.

An Archive Echo could be:

A remembered sentence from another person.
A younger version of the Subject.
An old belief.
A location speaking through reconstructed memory.
A discarded version of Administrator.
A voice generated by a particular artifact.

They appear only inside specific records:

ECHO RECOVERED: “You always restart when it becomes real.”

SOURCE UNKNOWN: “Stay for one more drink.”

CHILD RECORD: “The blanket is working.”

These are not autonomous entities. They are fragments. That keeps the world populated without expanding the central cast.
