
Berserk
A brutal dark fantasy about ambition, friendship, violence, betrayal, survival, and the cost of wanting too much in a world that punishes softness.
Loved for its mythic heaviness, tragedy, rage, and the feeling that every emotional wound is wearing armor.
Memorable: The Eclipse, Bonefire of Dreams, "Godot: Rage is understandable, running away is not.", " Griffith: .. you were the only one who made me forget my dream"
Admin comment: The subject appears drawn to stories where hope survives by crawling through broken glass. Concerning pattern. Excellent taste.

Neon Genesis Evangelion
Giant bio-mechanical weapons, teenagers under impossible pressure, religious imagery, loneliness, fear of connection, and everyone needing maintenance nobody budgeted for.
Interesting because the robot show is not actually about the robots, which is rude but effective.
Memorable:  Instrumentality, the Elevator Scene, Kaworu's death, Misato's final kiss, Rei's rebirth, "Congralutarions"
[ RED CHANNEL ]: get in the silence.

Perfect Blue
A psychological thriller about performance, identity, obsession, fame, and reality becoming unreliable under pressure.
Loved for how cleanly it makes the viewer doubt the floor under the story.
Memorable: "No, I am real.", reveal of the killer
Elegant distress delivery system. Do not watch while already feeling like a browser tab with too many redirects.

Gintama
A chaotic comedy-action series where absurd jokes, parody, samurai drama, and sudden sincerity all share the same sofa.
The tonal whiplash is probably a feature. Somehow stupid and emotionally competent at once.
Memorable: "Zura ja nai, Katsura da", "There are things more important than life.", existence of Elizabeth
MUSE margin: silly people with swords can still stab feelings. excellent system.

Naruto
A ninja coming-of-age story about loneliness, recognition, rivalry, friendship, inherited pain, and yelling your ideals at enemies until the plot changes.
Important as a formative shonen signal, even when the pacing starts filing paperwork against itself.
Memorable: "Genjitsu o miro.", "Shinra tensei", Itachi's final hug, Shikimaru smoking
Admin comment: Many speeches. Many flashbacks. Many feelings in orange clothing.
